Frequently Asked Questions

❓ How do I book my airport transfer?
Simply fill in the form below or chat with us on WhatsApp. We'll confirm your booking within 2 hours and send you all the details.
❓ What happens if my flight is delayed?
We monitor all flight arrivals in real time. Your driver will be there when you land, even if your flight is delayed. There are no extra charges for flight delays.
❓ How do I find my driver at the airport?
Your driver will be waiting at the arrivals hall holding a sign with your name. We'll also send you their contact details and vehicle information before your arrival.
❓ What type of vehicles do you use?
We use modern, air‑conditioned sedans and SUVs that comfortably accommodate up to 4 passengers with luggage. For larger groups, we can arrange minivans on request.
❓ Can I book a transfer from Watamu to the airport?
Absolutely. We offer transfers in both directions – from the airport to Watamu, and from Watamu back to the airport. Just let us know your departure time.
