Frequently Asked Questions — Watamu Packages

What is the minimum stay for these packages? ▾
All packages require a minimum of 5 nights. For shorter stays, we can create a custom quote – just ask.
Is the Tsavo East safari included in the Premium package? ▾
Yes – the Premium Safari & Beach package includes a 1-night stay in Tsavo East with full board, game drives, and park fees. You can extend this to 2 or 3 nights for an additional cost.
Can I upgrade the hotel in the Essential package? ▾
Absolutely. All packages are fully customisable. We can upgrade your accommodation, add meals, or include extra activities. Contact us for a tailored quote.
Are flights included? ▾
Flights are not included, but we can arrange them for you at competitive rates. We also offer transfers from Mombasa, Malindi, or Ukunda airports.
What is the best time to book? ▾
We recommend booking at least 3–6 months in advance, especially for peak season (December–February and July–October). Last‑minute availability is limited but possible.
